# Twemoji picture attribution

The 68 independent SVG illustrations in this directory are unmodified
Twemoji v17.0.1 graphics by Twitter and other contributors, licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). Commercial use
is permitted when attribution is supplied. The complete license text is in
`LICENSE-GRAPHICS` in this directory.

Source: https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji/tree/v17.0.1/assets/svg
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Each corresponding `data/picture_library.json` record identifies its exact
source file and SHA-256. The graphics are displayed with attribution in the
Guess Your Picture library and daily picture quiz.

These graphics depict general objects, places, food, fruit, vegetables and
animals. They are illustrations, not photographs. No celebrity portraits from
this source are used. The original v11.7 art remains intact.
